Privacy

Drafts and champion pools are saved in your browser. The companion reads champion select and match roles locally and sends champion identifiers and role assignments directly to this page. League credentials and player identities stay off the website.

After you connect, DraftEdge saves a reconnect key in your browser for this site. It lets a new tab reconnect to the companion on this PC. The key expires when the companion quits and is not included in Analytics.

After you lock in a champion, the companion requests statistics from LoLalytics for that champion, role, rank filter, and the revealed opposing champions and their assigned roles. DraftEdge loads game metadata and icons from Riot Data Dragon and item purchase rules from CommunityDragon.

DraftEdge uses Google Analytics to measure website visits. Google receives ordinary usage information such as browser/device information and may use analytics cookies. Our page tracking excludes URL queries, connection tokens, draft picks, pools, and searches. Enhanced measurement, Google signals, and advertising personalization are disabled for this setup. A browser Global Privacy Control signal skips Analytics.
